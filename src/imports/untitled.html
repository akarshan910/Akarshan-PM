I'm a PM at SugarFit (Curefit) — India's leading digital health platform. My work spans GLP-1 programs, CGM features, and clinical ops, serving hundreds of thousands of patients managing diabetes and weight.

I sit closest to the data. I query the warehouse directly, build the dashboards myself, and write the PRD in the same afternoon. The best product decisions come from actually understanding the numbers — not just asking someone to summarise them.

Before SugarFit I was a growth analyst — which means I'm fluent in funnels, obsessed with retention, and deeply allergic to vanity metrics.
