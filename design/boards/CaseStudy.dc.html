01

Objective

Improve order frequency — monthly puja orders per user — for Sri Mandir’s puja service.

Assumptions
  • Rituals are performed authentically; the challenge is proving it online.
  • The tech stack can support personalisation and localisation.
  • Booking history, inferred location and preferences are available.
  • Devotees want an ongoing spiritual connection, not only one-off help.
02

Who we’re solving for

Seven segments, three prioritised for their potential to raise monthly frequency.

Tech-savvy devoteesNRIs and distant devoteesSpecific needsNon-Hindi/English speakers
Priority 1
Time-constrained professionals, millennials and Gen Z

Receptive to convenient, recurring digital options like subscriptions.

Priority 2
Tech-informed devotees seeking regular practice

Already regular; benefit from streamlined flows and personalisation.

Priority 3
Non-technological regular devotees

Live on WhatsApp and Facebook; distrust online payments; strong repeat intent.

03

What’s broken today

From Google Play reviews and app usage.

Generic discovery

No recommendations from history, preferences or what’s popular nearby.

Low awareness

The puja service and its offerings are hard to discover.

Thin trust signals

Payment worries, no social proof, prasad quality, incorrect gotra.

Language barrier

Hindi and English only, leaving regional-language devotees out.

Learning curve

Multi-step flows unlike the WhatsApp and Facebook patterns users know.

No urgency cues

No timers on time-critical pujas; no “most picked” guidance.

06

Recommendations, from cold start

Collect details like gotra and important dates at the right moment — when a user taps a puja — and always explain the benefit.

No user data yet
  • Most-booked pujas in the user’s city
  • Upcoming and seasonal festivals
  • A “What’s trending” strip as social proof
When we know the user
  • Gotra, birthdays and anniversaries
  • Suggestions for important dates
  • A “For you” carousel from past bookings
