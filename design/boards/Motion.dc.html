Rules

Reduced motion
Same content, nothing moving
  • Follows the visitor’s prefers-reduced-motion setting.
  • No tilt, drift, layer shifts, speed-line aiming, rises, reveals, draws, sweeps, pulses or ring.
  • Floating previews stand still in their rest spots.
  • Colour and border changes on hover and focus stay, because they carry meaning.
  • The reading progress bar stays.
  • Nothing flashes, with or without the setting.
Performance
Cheap to run on a mid-range phone
  • Animate transform and opacity only.
  • Read the pointer once per frame and write it to CSS variables, not React state.
  • At most seven moving layers in the hero.
  • Pause loops and floating previews when the hero is off screen or the tab is hidden.
  • Freeze the speed lines if the frame rate drops below 45fps.
  • Covers load only when they are about to be seen.
