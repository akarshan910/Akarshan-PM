Layout and breakpoints

Fluid between breakpoints; designed at 1440, 834 and 390
Laptop≥ 1280

12 columns · 24 gutter · 56 margin · content max 1200. Hover reveals extra detail; nothing is hover-only.

iPad768 – 1279

Portrait: 8 columns · 20 gutter · 32 margin. Landscape (1024+) uses the laptop grid with 40 margins. Touch targets 44 px.

Mobile360 – 767

4 columns · 16 gutter · 20 margin. Single column of content, full-width primary actions, bottom sheets instead of popovers.
