01

Objective

Improve order frequency: monthly puja orders per user, for Sri Mandir’s puja service.

Assumptions
Rituals are performed authentically; the challenge is proving it online.
The tech stack can support personalisation and localisation.
Booking history, inferred location and preferences are available.
Devotees want an ongoing spiritual connection, not only one-off help.
02

Who we’re solving for

Seven segments, three prioritised for their potential to raise monthly frequency.

Priority 1
Time-constrained professionals, millennials and Gen Z
Receptive to convenient, recurring digital options like subscriptions.
Priority 2
Tech-informed devotees seeking regular practice
Already regular; benefit from streamlined flows and personalisation.
Priority 3
Non-technological regular devotees
Live on WhatsApp and Facebook; distrust online payments; strong repeat intent.
03

What’s broken today

From Google Play reviews and app usage.

01Generic discovery. No recommendations from history, preferences or what’s popular nearby.
02Low awareness. The puja service and its offerings are hard to discover.
03Thin trust signals. Payment worries, no social proof, prasad quality, incorrect gotra.
04Language barrier. Hindi and English only, leaving regional-language devotees out.
05Learning curve. Multi-step flows unlike the WhatsApp and Facebook patterns users know.
06No urgency cues. No timers on time-critical pujas; no “most picked” guidance.
04
