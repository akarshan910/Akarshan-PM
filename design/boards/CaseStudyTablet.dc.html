01

Objective

Improve order frequency — monthly puja orders per user — for Sri Mandir’s puja service.

Assumptions
  • Rituals are authentic; the challenge is proving it online.
  • The stack can support personalisation and localisation.
  • Booking history, location and preferences are available.
  • Devotees want ongoing connection, not only one-off help.
02

Who we’re solving for

Seven segments, three prioritised.

Priority 1
Time-constrained professionals and Gen Z
Priority 2
Devotees seeking regular practice
Priority 3
Non-technological regular devotees
03

What’s broken today

Generic discovery

No recommendations from history or location.

Low awareness

The puja service is hard to discover.

Thin trust signals

Payments, prasad quality, incorrect gotra.

Language barrier

Hindi and English only.

Learning curve

Unfamiliar multi-step flows.

No urgency cues

No timers or “most picked” guidance.
