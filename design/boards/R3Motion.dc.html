Depth, exploded

Seven layers. Far ones shift against the pointer and blur a little; near ones ride the tilt at real depth. Explode the stack to see how the hero is built.

  • Pointer in: tilt follows within ±6°, speed lines aim at the pointer.
  • Pointer out: the scene settles to a composed rest angle in 600ms.
  • Reduced motion: the scene stays at rest. Nothing tilts, drifts or aims.

Touch and tilt

  • Pointer: coarse (iPad, phones): no hover-only content. Tap once to preview, again to open.
  • Tilt: after one tap on “Enable tilt” (iOS asks permission), device orientation drives the parallax. Clamped to ±15°, smoothed (α 0.12).
  • No permission: a slow 9s ambient drift instead.
  • Scroll depth: panel contents move at 0.9× page speed, chips at 1.1×.
  • Mobile Chapter Spread: reads as a webtoon, one panel column.

Access

  • Reduced motion: no tilt, drift, aiming, pops or ring animation. Same content, composed rest pose.
  • No flashing: the impact pop brightens one small element, never the screen, and never more than 3 times a second.
  • Decorative layers (screentone, speed lines, halftone) are hidden from screen readers.
  • Contrast: text over halftone always sits in a solid caption box or panel (4.5:1 or more).
  • Keyboard parity: focus shows what hover shows.

Performance

  • Animate transform and opacity only. Filter on one element at a time.
  • Seven layers at most per scene, one rAF loop, pointer read once per frame.
  • In React, write pointer values to CSS variables on the scene (--mx, --my) from a ref. Don’t re-render per move.
  • will-change only while the pointer is inside a scene.
  • Auto-degrade: under 45fps for a second, freeze the speed lines and keep the tilt.
